  null R² and τ would rise.
Null (Σ-weighted, reference only): 1,000 channels as above with g_i = X_cᵀ z_i / √1110,

  generator, then projected by P_S⊥ (twice) and normalised. Weights directions toward the leading
  principal components of EVAL; reported as the 951st smallest of its 1,000 R² values on B

Column "R²": ordinary least squares of the channel on B = (1, x̃, ỹ, x̃², ỹ², x̃ỹ, z(λ₃), z(a₈)),
  built by design_basis with the two colour channels in K6's order — the basis of K6's step 3.
  x̃ = z(x), ỹ = z(y); z(·) = centre on the EVAL mean and divide by the population standard

  R² = 1 − SS_res / SS_tot, SS_tot about the channel's mean; in-sample on EVAL, n = 1,110, k = 8.
  Adjusted R² = 1 − (1 − R²)(n − 1)/(n − k), reported alongside.
Threshold τ: np.sort(R²)[950] — the 951st smallest of the deciding null's 1,000 R² values on B.
  Derivation (D13, N = 1,000, j = 951): a z that behaves like a null direction is exchangeable with
  the 1,000 null values, so it strictly exceeds the 951st smallest with probability at most
  50/1001 = 4.995% ≤ 5%. One candidate, tested once: no multiplicity correction. The error bounded
  is excluding a z no more predictable than a random direction of S⊥; admitting a predictable z
  is not bounded by this rule, as in K6.
Rule: z is admissible iff R²(z) ≤ τ. One test; no second candidate, no re-test. The margin
  R²(z) − τ is reported with the decision.
Positive control: h = tanh(d_esc) of dipole 1, as the operator computes it (λ₁ clipped; D4 where
  unclipped, D11 where clipped). Must satisfy R²(h) > τ on B. Limit: it tests detection of an
  exact, nonlinear redundancy with the position only.
Calibration controls (exact R² by construction, D7): ĥ = the OLS fit of z(h) on B; n⊥ = the first
  deciding-null channel, residualised on B; m_ρ = a·z(ĥ) + b·z(n⊥), a² / (a² + b²) = ρ, built by
  calibration_control. ρ_in = τ / 2 (must be admissible) and ρ_out = (1 + τ) / 2 (must not be);
  each measured R² must equal its ρ within 1e-9. Tolerance: the fitted values and residuals of a
  float64 least-squares fit on 8 columns have forward error ≈ cond(B)·ε, ε = 2.2e-16; the run
  requires cond(B) ≤ 1e5 (bound ≈ 2.2e-11, about forty-five times below 1e-9) and reports cond(B)
  and every deviation. What they detect: a wrong regression, a wrong basis, a reversed
  comparison. What they cannot detect: a wrong τ.
Construction checks (reported always):
  rank: σ_min(M) ≥ 1e-6, so S has dimension 4 (D14's condition).
  survival: ‖P_S⊥w₂‖ / ‖w₂‖ ≥ 1e-6, so u is defined and the second pass is effective (D14 note).
  The value 1e-6 is the operator's collinear tolerance (eps_collinear default,
  traianus/geometry/polar_projector.py:58), reused by analogy — the operator's test is absolute
  on an unnormalised difference, these two are relative; declared, not derived. What they allow:
  a backward-stable QR perturbs S's orientation by about ε·σ_max(M)/σ_min(M), and dividing by the
  survival ratio turns that into a relative error of u within S⊥ of about ε·σ_max(M)/(σ_min(M) ·
  survival) — ≈ 4e-4 if both sat at their limits. The run reports σ_min(M), σ_max(M), the

  orthogonality of u: max over the four columns s of M of |⟨u, s⟩| ≤ 1e-12, and |‖u‖ − 1| ≤

  Why 1e-12: each computed 384-term inner product carries at most γ₃₈₄ = 384·(ε/2) /
  (1 − 384·(ε/2)) ≈ 4.3e-14 of rounding per unit of ‖a‖‖b‖ (Higham, Accuracy and Stability of

  normalisation, a handful of such operations, well below 1e-12. The checks detect a wrong span
  (a spanning vector left out leaves |⟨·, s⟩| of the order of that vector's angle to the
  direction), not a precision limit; the measured maxima are reported.

  bound of its exact value, so rows with equal exact z have a population sd of at most the bound;
  a spread at or below it is rounding, not measurable, and z_score would refuse it.
