Direction u: S = span{ĉ₁, w₁, w₃, p₈}, the four directions whose projections are already shown

  Adjusted R² = 1 − (1 − R²)(n − 1)/(n − k), reported alongside.
Threshold τ: np.sort(R²)[950] — the 951st smallest of the deciding null's 1,000 R² values on B.
  Derivation (D13): a z that behaves like a null direction is exchangeable with the 1,000 null
  values, so it exceeds the 951st smallest with probability ≤ 50/1001 = 4.995% ≤ 5%. One
  candidate, tested once: no multiplicity correction. The error bounded is excluding a z that is
  no more predictable than a random direction of S⊥; admitting a predictable z is not bounded by
  this rule, as in K6.
Rule: z is admissible iff R²(z) ≤ τ. One test; there is no second candidate and no re-test.
Positive control: h = tanh(d_esc) of dipole 1, as the operator computes it (λ₁ clipped; D4 where
  unclipped, D11 where clipped). Must satisfy R²(h) > τ on B. Limit: it tests detection of an
  exact, nonlinear redundancy with the position only.
Calibration controls (exact R² by construction, D7): ĥ = the OLS fit of z(h) on B; n⊥ = the first
  deciding-null channel, residualised on B; m_ρ = a·z(ĥ) + b·z(n⊥), a² / (a² + b²) = ρ, built by
  calibration_control. ρ_in = τ / 2 (must be admissible) and ρ_out = (1 + τ) / 2 (must not be);
  each measured R² must equal its ρ within 1e-9. Tolerance: float64 least squares on 8 columns
  has forward error ≈ cond(B)·ε, ε = 2.2e-16; the run requires cond(B) ≤ 1e5 (bound ≈ 2e-11,
  fifty times below 1e-9) and reports cond(B) and every deviation. What they detect: a wrong
  regression, a wrong basis, a reversed comparison. What they cannot detect: a wrong τ.
Construction checks on u (reported always):
  rank: σ_min of [ĉ₁, w₁/‖w₁‖, w₃/‖w₃‖, p₈/‖p₈‖] ≥ 1e-6, so S has dimension 4 (D14's condition).
  survival: ‖P_S⊥w₂‖ / ‖w₂‖ ≥ 1e-6, so u is defined.
  The tolerance 1e-6 is the operator's collinear tolerance (eps_collinear default,
  traianus/geometry/polar_projector.py:58), reused for the same kind of degeneracy; declared, not
  derived.
  orthogonality: max over the four normalised spanning vectors s of |⟨u, s⟩| ≤ 1e-12, and
  |‖u‖ − 1| ≤ 1e-12. Each computed 384-term inner product carries at most γ₃₈₄ = 384·(ε/2) /
  (1 − 384·(ε/2)) ≈ 4.3e-14 of rounding per unit of ‖a‖‖b‖ (Higham, Accuracy and Stability of
  Numerical Algorithms, 2nd ed., §3.1); u passes through two projection passes and a
  normalisation, a handful of such operations, well below 1e-12. The check is a detector of a
  wrong span (a spanning vector left out leaves |⟨u, s⟩| of the order of its angle to w₂), not a
  precision claim; the measured values are reported.
  spread: sd(z) on EVAL > γ₃₈₄ · max‖v‖ over EVAL (‖u‖ = 1): each computed z lies within that
  bound of its exact value, so rows with equal exact z have a population sd of at most the bound;
  a spread at or below it is rounding, not measurable.
