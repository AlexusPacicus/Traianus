# K6 — colour channel independence

**Question.** In a per-epoch geodetic frame, can three dimensions beyond position be shown as
colour without repeating the position or each other?

**Refuter.** No candidate is admissible under the rule below. That is a decision point for the
author, not a cue to change the candidates.

## Instrument audit record (revision 3)

```
Instrument audit — K6, colour channel predictability (revision 3)

Scope: the column measures second-order predictability of a channel from the position and from
  the channels selected before it — not full statistical independence.
Data: definitions.md (2,221 vectors, 8-axis basis). X_c = the 2,221 × 384 data matrix centred on
  its column means.
Frame (one per epoch): rank â_1…â_8 by the mean over the 2,221 vectors of ⟨v, â_k⟩, descending;
  ties broken by axis id, ascending. Anchor ĉ₁ = rank 1. Poles are always the normalised â.
  Each dipole's c_A is the higher-ranked and c_B the lower-ranked axis of its pair: dipole 1 =
  ranks (2, 3), dipole 2 = (4, 5), dipole 3 = (6, 7); rank 8 left over.
Collinear fallback (‖P⊥c_A − P⊥c_B‖ < 1e-6, the operator's default), logged per dipole:
  dipole 1 on the fallback → the instrument is invalid and the run stops (x and the positive
  control are undefined as a contrast); dipole 2 or 3 on the fallback → that dipole's λ is not
  a candidate.
Position: x = λ₁, y = ⟨v, ĉ₁⟩.
Candidates, in preference order:
  λ₂; λ₃; ⟨v, P⊥â_8⟩ (projected like every other channel, orthogonal to ĉ₁);
  l = 1/(1 + Var) of the 8 projections ⟨v, â_k⟩, population variance (ddof = 0). l shares
  inputs with the position by construction: its terms include ⟨v, ĉ₁⟩ = y and the two dipole-1
  axes that define x. It is kept, last in order, and the rule decides.
Clipping: every λ-type channel (x, λ₂, λ₃, the null) enters the regression unclipped,
  ⟨v, w⟩ / ‖w‖²; clipping is a rendering step, not information. The fraction of vectors that
  would clip is reported per arm, descriptive only.
Null (matched, deciding): 1,000 channels ⟨v, w_i⟩ / ‖w_i‖² with w_i = P⊥g_i,
  g_i = X_cᵀ z_i / √2221, z_i = rng.standard_normal(2221), so Cov(g_i) = Σ, the population
  covariance of the corpus. rng = numpy.random.default_rng(20260918) (PCG64); z_1…z_1000 drawn
  in that order, before anything else. Why matched: the λ channels project onto
  P⊥(â_i − â_j), orthogonal to ĉ₁ and inside the span where the corpus varies; w_i shares both.
Null (uniform, reference only): 1,000 unit vectors, rng.standard_normal(384) normalised, drawn
  after the matched null from the same generator. Reported, never used by the rule.
Column "R²_s" (selection step s = 1, 2, 3): ordinary least squares of the channel on
  B_s = (1, x, y, x², y², xy, c_1, …, c_{s−1}), with c_j the channels already selected; centred
  R² = 1 − SS_res / SS_tot, SS_tot about the channel's mean; in-sample, n = 2,221. Adjusted R²
  reported alongside.
Threshold τ_s: the 98.75th percentile of the matched null's 1,000 R² values on the same basis
  B_s, recomputed at every step. Derivation: four candidates tested, familywise error 5%, so
  per candidate 0.05 / 4 = 1.25% (Bonferroni): the chance of excluding at least one truly
  unpredictable candidate by accident is ≤ 5% across the four.
Rule (sequential): at step s, the first remaining candidate in preference order with
  R²_s ≤ τ_s is selected as c_s; a candidate that fails at step s is discarded. Fewer than
  three selected → the missing channels are constant.
Positive control: h = tanh(d_esc) of dipole 1, as the operator computes it (λ₁ clipped).
  Must satisfy R²_1(h) > τ_1. Limit: when λ₁ is not clipped, d_esc² = 1 − y² − ‖v_dipole,1‖²·x²,
  so h tests detection of an exact, nonlinear redundancy only.
Calibration controls (exact R² by construction): ĥ = the OLS fit of z(h) on B_1; n⊥ = the first
  matched-null channel, residualised on B_1. For target ρ, m_ρ = a·z(ĥ) + b·z(n⊥) with
  a² / (a² + b²) = ρ; since n⊥ ⊥ B_1 in-sample, R²_1(m_ρ) = ρ exactly. Two controls:
  ρ_in = τ_1 / 2 (must be admissible) and ρ_out = (1 + τ_1) / 2 (must not be).
  Each measured R²_1 must equal its ρ within 1e-9. These detect a wrong regression or a
  misplaced threshold in the implementation; they are not meant to fail on unusual data.

Same thing in every arm?   Every candidate, control and null channel on the same vectors, frame,
                           unclipped form, basis B_s and regression. Declared difference: l is not
                           a projection and shares inputs with the position.
Leakage?                   The frame is fitted on the corpus it describes — by design, it is the
                           epoch's frame; no label or ground truth is read.
Comparable arms?           Valid only if dipole 1 is not on the fallback, R²_1(h) > τ_1, and both
                           calibration controls land on the stated side of τ_1 at their exact ρ.
                           If any fails, the instrument is invalid and no candidate result is
                           used. Geodetic axes are not random: if they come out more predictable
                           than the null, that is a result for the author, not a reason to relax
                           the rule.
Text matches code?         Checked at review (phase 2).
Reviewed by:               pending (revision 3).
```
