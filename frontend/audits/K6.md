# K6 — colour channel independence

**Question.** In a per-epoch geodetic frame, can three dimensions beyond position be shown as
colour without repeating it?

**Refuter.** No candidate is admissible under the rule below. That is a decision point for the
author, not a cue to change the candidates.

## Instrument audit record (revision 2)

```
Instrument audit — K6, colour channel independence (revision 2)

Data: definitions.md (2,221 vectors, 8-axis basis).
Frame (one per epoch): rank â_1…â_8 by the mean over the 2,221 vectors of ⟨v, â_k⟩, descending;
  ties broken by axis id, ascending. Anchor ĉ₁ = rank 1. Each dipole's c_A is the higher-ranked
  and c_B the lower-ranked axis of its pair: dipole 1 = ranks (2, 3), dipole 2 = (4, 5),
  dipole 3 = (6, 7); rank 8 left over.
Collinear fallback: logged per dipole (‖P⊥c_A − P⊥c_B‖ < 1e-6, the operator's default). A dipole
  on the fallback yields no admissible candidate: its λ is no longer a contrast of its two axes.
Position: x = λ₁, y = ⟨v, ĉ₁⟩.
Candidates, in preference order:
  λ₂; λ₃; ⟨v, â_8⟩;
  l = 1/(1 + Var) of the 8 projections ⟨v, â_k⟩, population variance (ddof = 0). l shares
  inputs with the position by construction: its terms include ⟨v, ĉ₁⟩ = y and the two dipole-1
  axes that define x. It is kept, last in order, and the rule decides.
Clipping: every λ-type channel (x, λ₂, λ₃, the null) enters the regression unclipped,
  ⟨v, w⟩ / ‖w‖²; clipping is a rendering step, not information. The fraction of vectors that
  would clip is reported per arm, descriptive only.
Null (matched, deciding): 1,000 directions w = P⊥g, g ~ N(0, Σ), Σ the population covariance of
  the 2,221 vectors; channel ⟨v, w⟩ / ‖w‖². Generator numpy.random.default_rng(20260918)
  (PCG64), the 1,000 draws taken once, in order. Why matched: λ₂ and λ₃ project onto
  P⊥(â_i − â_j), orthogonal to ĉ₁ and inside the span where the corpus varies; w shares both.
  ⟨v, â_8⟩ and l are judged against the same null.
Null (uniform, reference only): 1,000 unit vectors uniform on S^383 (normalised Gaussians, same
  generator, drawn after the matched null). Reported, never used by the rule.
Column "R²": ordinary least squares of the channel on (1, x, y, x², y², xy), with intercept;
  R² = 1 − SS_res / SS_tot, SS_tot about the channel's mean (centred), in-sample, n = 2,221,
  6 parameters. Adjusted R² reported alongside (in-sample inflation ≈ p/n ≈ 0.002).
Rule: admissible iff R² ≤ the 95th percentile of the matched null's 1,000 R² values; colour =
  the first three admissible candidates in order; fewer than three → the missing channels are
  constant.
Positive control: h = tanh(d_esc) of dipole 1, as the operator computes it (λ₁ clipped).
  Limit: when λ₁ is not clipped, d_esc² = 1 − y² − ‖v_dipole,1‖²·x², so h is tanh of the square
  root of a polynomial the regression basis already contains. It shows the instrument detects an
  exact redundancy, not a partial one near the threshold.
Graded controls: m_α = α·z(h) + (1 − α)·z(n₁), α ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75}; z = standardisation over the
  2,221 vectors; n₁ = the first matched-null channel.

Same thing in every arm?   Every candidate, control and null channel on the same vectors, frame,
                           unclipped form and regression. Declared difference: l is not a
                           projection and shares inputs with the position.
Leakage?                   The frame is fitted on the corpus it describes — by design, it is the
                           epoch's frame; no label or ground truth is read.
Comparable arms?           Valid only if R²(h) > the matched null's 95th percentile AND
                           R²(m_0.25) < R²(m_0.5) < R²(m_0.75). If either fails, the instrument
                           is invalid and no candidate result is used. Geodetic axes are not
                           random: if they come out more redundant than the null, that is a
                           result for the author, not a reason to relax the rule.
Text matches code?         Checked at review (phase 2).
Reviewed by:               pending (revision 2).
```
